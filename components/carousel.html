Components

Carousel

Shows a project’s mockups one at a time in a 3:2 frame, with a small Berry arrow group centered underneath. It never moves on its own; visitors step through the slides with the arrows, a swipe, or the arrow keys.

Live Demo

Previous is disabled on the first slide and Next on the last, so the arrows always show where you are.

Accessibility Guidelines

  • 2.2.2No autoplay. Slides only change when the visitor asks, so nothing moves while someone is reading, and there is no motion to pause.
  • 1.3.1Label the carousel as a region with aria-roledescription="carousel" and an aria-label naming its content. Each slide is a group labelled “1 of 3,” “2 of 3” and so on (WAI-ARIA carousel pattern).
  • 4.1.2The arrows are real <button>s with hidden text (“Previous slide,” “Next slide”) and aria-controls pointing at the slides. The chevron icons are aria-hidden.
  • 4.1.3A polite live region announces “Slide 2 of 3” after each move, so screen reader users know the change worked.
  • 1.1.1Every slide image needs alt text that says what the image shows, not just “mockup.” For a chart or table, sum up its main finding.
  • 2.5.8The arrow buttons are drawn at 30px but each has a 44px invisible tap area, and they are 4px apart, above the 24px minimum target size.
  • 1.4.11The arrow buttons contrast with the page (Berry is 6.4:1 on Warm Linen) and the chevrons with their button (6.4:1).
  • 2.1.1Everything works from the keyboard: Tab to the arrows and press Enter or Space. When an arrow becomes disabled at the end, focus moves to the other arrow instead of being lost.
  • 2.3.3With reduced motion turned on, slides switch instantly instead of sliding.
  • 1.3.2Don’t put essential information only in a carousel. Slides after the first are easy to miss, so repeat key points in the page text.
